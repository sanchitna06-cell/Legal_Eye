1. UNDERSTAND THE PRODUCT

The project is called:

LEGAL EYE

Legal Eye is a web platform designed to maintain and organize records of legal matters, court cases, case histories, and related legal information.

The platform also incorporates an AI capability intended to help users understand legal cases by:

summarizing lengthy case information

explaining case history

helping users navigate case-related information

potentially surfacing important details from a case

reducing the effort required to understand lengthy legal records

The AI should therefore feel like an intelligent legal research assistant, not like a chatbot that has been randomly attached to a legal website.

The core visual identity should revolve around:

LAW × COURTS × RECORDS × RESEARCH × HISTORY × INTELLIGENCE
